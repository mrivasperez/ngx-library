Keyboard Shortcuts

How to customize shortcuts

Click the "Record" button next to any action, then press the key combination you want to assign. The shortcut will be captured automatically. You can remove shortcuts by clicking the remove button.

Tip: Try combinations like Ctrl+K, Alt+H, or Shift+A for better accessibility.

Test Your Shortcuts

Once you've assigned shortcuts, try pressing them anywhere in the application! The shortcuts will work from any page and execute their corresponding actions.

For example, if you assign Ctrl+H to "Go to Library", pressing Ctrl+H will navigate you to the main page.
